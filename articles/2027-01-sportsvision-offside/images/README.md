The SVGs are the project's own diagrams, copied unchanged from `docs/architecture/diagrams/svg/` in [sportsvision](https://github.com/ricardocunha/sportsvision) (Apache-2.0), where their [archify](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify) sources live. They follow the reader's light or dark theme. The PNGs are light-theme renders of the same SVGs, for Medium, which does not render SVG: upload the PNG there by hand. Labels are in English in both the EN and PT-BR articles. No video frames or footage-derived images are included.
