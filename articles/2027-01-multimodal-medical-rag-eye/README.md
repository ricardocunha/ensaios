# Multimodal RAG for eye scans

**Status:** draft · **Branch:** `article/2027-01-multimodal-medical-rag-eye` · **Target date:** 2027-01-08

> **Research demo, not a medical device.** Nothing here diagnoses anyone or supports a clinical decision. The numbers measure agreement with the labels of one public dataset, not diagnostic accuracy.

Retrieval-augmented classification of retinal fundus photos: BiomedCLIP embeddings in a local Qdrant index, evaluated on RFMiD (public, CC BY 4.0). See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md), [code/docs/DATA.md](code/docs/DATA.md) and [code/docs/RESULTS.md](code/docs/RESULTS.md).
